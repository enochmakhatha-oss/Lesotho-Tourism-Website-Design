Why visit Lesotho?

Lesotho is the only country in the world entirely above 1,000 metres. The Maloti and Drakensberg mountains shape life here: shepherds wrap themselves in Basotho blankets, ponies are a normal way to travel, and every valley has a story.

It is quiet, safe to explore with a local guide, and easy to reach from Johannesburg, Bloemfontein or Durban.

  • Pony trek between villages
  • Abseil beside a 192 metre waterfall
  • Ski in winter, hike in summer
  • Learn the history of King Moshoeshoe I
